How to use
  1. Identify the colorable parts of your model (red here)
  2. Sample the color of your prop's tintable area and get the target color (e.g., from a screenshot)
  3. Type/paste both into the fields above, click Calculate, enable normalization if necessary, and copy the calculated value
  4. Paste this into your prop's tint color setting
  5. The model now matches your target color!
